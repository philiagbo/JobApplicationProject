<objective>
Make the profile pay off twice. First, jobs that mention the person's skills rank a little higher and say why ("Matches 3 of your skills: Excel, SQL, Python"), without salary losing its place as the heaviest rule. Second, any job can produce an editable cover letter, filled from the profile and that job, through `GET /api/jobs/<id>/cover-letter`.

Purpose: covers PROF-05 and decisions D-07, D-08, D-09 and D-15 to D-17. It lifts the Phase 1 D-05 deferral now that a profile exists (plan 03-03). Phase 5 autofill will call `core.cover_letter` when a form has a cover-letter field.
Output: the skills rule and `cover_letter` in `api/core.py`; profile-aware `insert_jobs` and `rescore_new`, plus `get_job`, in `api/db.py`; the cover-letter route and the profile re-score in `api/app.py`; the new `api/cover_letter.txt`; tests.
</objective>
