<objective>
Ship the Gmail source end to end, minus the four site parsers. The person connects Gmail through `PUT /api/gmail` (App Password kept in `keyring`, address in SQLite). The watcher polls Gmail on its own `gmail_poll_minutes` cadence over read-only IMAP, searches each alert sender since the last poll, hands each email to the site's parser, and inserts the jobs like any other source. Zero-job alerts warn in the `gmail` status.

Purpose: covers GMAIL-01 and D-01 to D-05, D-08, D-10. The four site parsers need the person's real sample emails (D-06), so `alerts.ALERTS` starts empty here and plan 04-05 adds the four entries; tests register a test site through the same registry. This plan leads with its tracer slice (Task 1).
Decision recorded (RESEARCH open question 2): a manual `POST /api/fetch` with no career fields runs Gmail alone when Gmail is connected, because D-04 makes Gmail independent of career fields. With neither, the existing 400 stays word for word.
Output: `api/alerts.py`, two pure helpers in `api/core.py`, two raw-settings helpers in `api/db.py`, the Gmail tick in `api/watcher.py`, the `/api/gmail` routes in `api/app.py`, and `api/test_gmail.py`.

Require scheme (lowercased) in http or https and a non-empty hostname. Unless ALLOW_PRIVATE_HOSTS is true, return False for hostname "localhost" or any hostname ending in ".localhost", and, when the hostname parses with ipaddress.ip_address, for any address that is private, loopback, link-local, reserved, multicast or unspecified.
