<objective>
Give every job its full description in the background. New and existing jobs start `pending`; a details worker classifies each one with no network (skip LinkedIn/Indeed and excluded titles, keep long descriptions), fetches the rest one every 5 seconds over urllib, reads the page's JSON-LD `JobPosting` or its largest text block, and saves the text. New jobs are re-summarised and re-scored; queued and dismissed jobs keep their score. Blocked pages are marked `blocked` ("open listing to read").

Purpose: covers DETAIL-01 and the detection and marking half of DETAIL-02 (D-11 to D-13, D-16 to D-18). Plan 04-06 adds the single Playwright retry between "urllib could not read it" and "blocked", completing DETAIL-02. Task 1 is the D-17 one-way checkpoint; Task 2 is the tracer.
Output: the guarded migration and two DB helpers in `api/db.py`, `details_plan` in `api/core.py`, the fetch and extraction functions in `api/sources.py`, the worker thread in `api/watcher.py`, `api/test_details.py`, and test isolation updates in `api/test_watcher.py`.
