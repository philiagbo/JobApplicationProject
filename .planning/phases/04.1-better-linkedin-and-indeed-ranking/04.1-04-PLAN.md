<objective>
Stop the same alert job from appearing twice when its links carry no job ID. Indeed job-match emails, Totaljobs invites and sponsored Indeed links use opaque tracker URLs, so the URL dedupe cannot see that two emails point at one job. These jobs get a title+company key; a newcomer with a key that already exists is dropped, and the older row keeps its status. Rows already stored before this phase are keyed once at start-up, so the first poll after the upgrade does not add another copy.

Purpose: covers the CONTEXT "Duplicate merging" item (Claude's Discretion), using the suggested default: merge only among ID-less alert jobs, on normalised title plus the D-04 company normalisation, keep the older row and its status, no cross-source merge; plus the start-up backfill the person decided on 2026-11-12 (key only the oldest of colliding stored rows, never delete, never change a status). Builds on `core.company_key` from 04.1-01. Which links are ID-less is one URL rule, `core.idless_alert(source, url)`, used by both the insert and the backfill. Task 1 is the tracer (two Indeed job-match emails through the fake IMAP to one row in GET /api/jobs); Task 2 adds the backfill and pins the rules, test-first.
Output: `idless_alert` and `dedupe_key` in `api/core.py`; the column, index, insert change and backfill in `api/db.py`; updated ponytail comments in `api/alerts.py`; tests in `api/test_gmail.py` and `api/test_details.py`.
