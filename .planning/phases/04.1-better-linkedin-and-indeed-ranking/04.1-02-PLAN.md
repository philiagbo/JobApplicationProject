<objective>
Score jobs that only have an alert snippet fairly and label them. A job is "partial info" when its description is under 500 characters and it was not filled from Reed or pasted. Such a job gets a con with a next step and a `summary.partial_info` flag the future UI can show as a badge. When the person has skills in their profile, a partial job's skills rule gets at least half the cap, so a strong title, salary or work-mode match is not buried for lacking text.

Purpose: covers D-09, D-10, D-11 and D-12, and the UI-SPEC binding strings and flag (E1/E3/E4 partial truths). Builds on the "reed" status and `db.save_reed_match` from 04.1-01. Task 1 is the tracer (snippet job to GET /api/jobs, partial then filled); Task 2 adds the neutral skills credit test-first.
Output: `is_partial`, `PARTIAL_CON` and the score rules in `api/core.py`; the read-time flag in `api/db.py`; tests in `api/test_core.py` and `api/test_api.py`.

Recorded choices: the literal D-09 rule is kept, so a page-fetched "ok" description of 200 to 499 characters also counts as partial (RESEARCH Pitfall 8 and Open Question 2; the person can paste). A queued job matched from Reed keeps its stored cons (D-05/D-13 freeze) while its flag turns false on read; this is expected, not a bug. The empty-profile rule for the skills credit is D-10a in 04.1-CONTEXT.md (user-confirmed 2026-11-12; was RESEARCH assumption A6).
