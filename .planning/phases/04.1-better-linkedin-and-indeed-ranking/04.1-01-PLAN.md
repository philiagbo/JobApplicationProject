<objective>
Give LinkedIn and Indeed alert jobs a real description from the Reed API. The details worker, which Phase 4 told to skip these jobs, now asks Reed once per job: up to two keyword searches, a strict local company and title match, then one job-details request for the matched ad. On a match the job gets Reed's full text and is re-scored if it is still new. With no key, no match or a Reed error, the job ends "blocked" and is not retried. LinkedIn and Indeed are still never contacted.

Purpose: covers D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04 and D-05, and keeps the Phase 4 D-18 rule. The ROADMAP scope line predates CONTEXT.md: employer careers/ATS lookups are deferred and location is only a tie-breaker (D-01, D-04). The spec-less probe fallback was skipped because this phase has no requirement IDs to probe (visible skip).
Output: Reed lookup in `api/sources.py`, match rules and the "lookup" plan value in `api/core.py`, `save_reed_match` and the start-up update in `api/db.py`, the worker branch in `api/watcher.py`, tests in `api/test_details.py` and `api/test_core.py`.

What the person will notice: LinkedIn/Indeed jobs found while no Reed key is saved end "blocked" ("open listing to read") and are not looked up again after a key is added later (D-03, one try); those jobs keep the paste option (04.1-03). Only jobs that arrive after the key is saved, or old rows Phase 4 left "skipped", get a lookup.

Task 1 is the tracer (one LinkedIn job through the worker to a Reed-filled job in GET /api/jobs). Task 2 tightens the match rules and adds the Reed pro, test-first. Task 3 covers the no-key, no-match, failure and start-up paths and carries the live Reed check.
